# Portal Guide

How to use the PHILSAR Cattle Reproductive Portal. The pages are in the left sidebar: Announcements, Dashboard, Learning Modules, Decision Support, Virtual Meetings, Our Community and My Profile, plus the Admin Panel for Admins and Sub Admins. On a phone, open the sidebar with the menu button in the top bar.

## Finding your way around the portal

- Announcements: the first page you see, with the photo banner, the latest announcements (click one to read it), Upcoming Seminars and Quick Access shortcuts.
- Dashboard: a summary of your herd, assessments, seminars and learning progress.
- Learning Modules: lessons on cattle reproduction.
- Decision Support: check whether a cow is ready for breeding.
- Virtual Meetings: live seminars, recordings, meeting minutes and certificates.
- Our Community: about PHILSAR (its mission, vision, KISSPA core values, strategic objectives and founding president).
- My Profile: your name, organization, photo, password and email address.
- Top bar: the search box ("Search modules, topics…") finds Learning Modules by name, the EN / TL buttons switch the portal between English and Tagalog, the bell shows your notifications, and the 👤 button opens My Profile.
- "🚪 Sign Out" is at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Creating an account and signing in

- On the sign-in page, click "Create one here". Fill in First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Password, Role (Livestock Manager, Farmer, Veterinarian or Extension Worker) and Organization / Farm (optional), then click "Create Account".
- The email address must be real and working. It is checked as you type, and if it looks like a typo the portal suggests a fix ("Did you mean …?").
- The password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character. The checklist under the box shows which rules are met.
- Then open the verification link sent to your email (it works for 24 hours) and sign in with your email and password. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or click "Resend verification email".
- After 5 wrong passwords within 5 minutes, signing in is blocked for a few minutes. Wait, then try again.
- A deactivated account can't sign in. Contact an administrator.

## Forgot password or changing your password

- Forgot your password: on the sign-in page, click "Forgot password?", enter your email and click "Send Reset Link". Open the link in the email within 1 hour and set a new password.
- To change it while signed in: go to My Profile, fill in Current Password and New Password under "Change Password", then click "Save Changes". The new password must meet the same rules as when signing up.

## Updating your profile, photo or email address

- On My Profile you can edit your First Name, Last Name and Organization / Farm Address. Click "Save Changes" when done.
- Profile picture: click your photo to upload a new one, or use the remove button to delete it.
- Email address: it can't be edited in the form. Under "Change Email Address", enter the New Email Address and your Current Password and click "Send Verification Link", then open the link sent to the new address within 24 hours. The email changes only after you confirm it.
- Only an administrator can change your role. When that happens you get a notification in the bell and by email.
- The Learning Summary on My Profile shows your Modules Completed, Seminars Registered and DSS Assessments.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows your notifications, and a red number on it shows how many are unread. Click the bell to open the list. For example, you're notified when an administrator changes your role (you also get an email about it).

## Studying the Learning Modules

- Open Learning Modules and pick a topic to filter the list: All Topics, Anatomy & Physiology, Breeds & Breeding, Estrus Cycle & Detection, Reproductive Biotechnology, Gestation & Pregnancy or Reproductive Health.
- Each module card shows how many lessons it has, about how long it takes, and your progress.
- Click a module to open it. The "Module Structure" list beside the lesson lets you jump to any lesson.
- When you finish a lesson, click "✓ Mark Lesson as Complete" ("✕ Unmark Lesson" undoes it). Your progress shows on the module card, the Dashboard and My Profile.
- "← Back to Modules" returns to the list. You can also find a module with the search box in the top bar.

## Running a breeding assessment (Decision Support)

1. Open Decision Support.
2. Fill in the cow's details: Cattle ID / Tag Number (for example B-041), Reproductive History (Successful Previous Calving, First Breeding or History of Infertility), Age (years), Body Condition Score (1–9), Days Since Last Calving (not needed for a first breeding), Estrus Indicators (select all you've seen: Standing Heat, Mounting Others, Clear Discharge, Swollen Vulva, or None Observed) and Current Health Status.
3. Click "🧬 Evaluate Breeding Readiness".

The result shows "✅ Ready for Breeding" or "⚠️ Postpone Breeding Recommended", the Recommended Technique, an Evaluation Checklist that marks each condition ✅ or ❌, and written breeding guidance based on the Learning Modules.

Every assessment is saved. The cow's tag number is added to your herd (Total Cattle on the Dashboard), and the result appears under Recent DSS Assessments on the Dashboard.

## How Decision Support decides if a cow is ready

A cow is "Ready for Breeding" only when all of these are true:
- Her age is 2 to 8 years.
- Her Body Condition Score is 5 to 7.
- At least one heat sign was seen (not "None Observed").
- At least 45 days have passed since her last calving (the voluntary waiting period). This is skipped for a first breeding.
- Her health status is "Healthy — no issues" or "Minor health issue — treated".
- Her reproductive history isn't "History of Infertility". A repeat breeder should have a veterinary reproductive exam first.

Recommended Technique: Artificial Insemination (AI) when she's ready and Standing Heat or Clear Discharge was seen, Natural Mating when she's ready with other heat signs only, and otherwise "Postpone Breeding". If she isn't ready, the ❌ items in the Evaluation Checklist show what to fix before checking her again.

## Your Dashboard

- The cards at the top show Total Cattle, Ready for Breeding, Modules Completed and Seminars Registered. Click a card to see the list behind it.
- Total Cattle counts the cattle you've assessed in Decision Support (each tag number once).
- Recent DSS Assessments lists your latest results, and "Run new" opens Decision Support.
- Upcoming Seminars lists what's coming up, with RSVP buttons. Learning Center Overview shows your module progress, and Quick Actions are shortcuts to the main pages.

## Joining a seminar or meeting (Virtual Meetings)

- Open Virtual Meetings. "All Sessions" lists the seminars and meetings, and you can filter it with All, Upcoming or Recorded.
- To register, click "RSVP" on an upcoming session. It changes to "✓ Registered" and is listed under My Registered Sessions. You have to RSVP before you can join.
- When it's live, click "Join Live" on the session (or "🎥 Join Live Stream" in the Live Now banner). The video room opens inside the portal using Jitsi Meet, so there's nothing to install and no extra account to create. Use Toggle Mic, Toggle Camera and Toggle Chat, and click "Leave Meeting" to exit.
- The Upcoming Seminars lists on the Announcements page and the Dashboard have RSVP buttons too.
- Some Regular Meetings are only open to certain roles, so you may not see every meeting.

## Watching a recording or reading the minutes

- For a session that has ended, click "Watch Replay" in Virtual Meetings (the Recorded filter lists them). If a recording was uploaded, click "▶ Watch Recording".
- The Meeting Minutes appear there once the seminar ends. Click "Download PDF" to save them.
- Admins, Sub Admins and Secretaries write the minutes during the meeting with "📝 Write Minutes", then click "Save Minutes".

## Getting a seminar certificate

- Certificates are given for Seminars, not Regular Meetings.
- Attend the live seminar in the portal's video room for at least the required time: 30 minutes, unless the administrators set a different number. Your time in the room is counted automatically.
- After the seminar ends, go to Virtual Meetings and click "🎓 Certificate" on that seminar to download your certificate as a PDF.
- No button? The seminar may not have ended yet, you may not have stayed long enough, or it was a Regular Meeting. An administrator can also grant a certificate manually.
- If an administrator revokes your certificate, the seminar shows "Certificate revoked" and it can't be downloaded.

## Checking that a certificate is real (QR code)

Every certificate has its own Certificate ID (in the form XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) and a QR code. Scanning the QR code with a phone camera opens a verification page that says "✓ Valid certificate" or "✗ Revoked certificate", and shows who it was awarded to, the seminar, its host and date, and when it was issued. If an administrator revokes the certificate later, the same QR code shows it as revoked, and if it's granted again, it shows as valid again.

## Chatting with PHILSARBot

- The round chat button in the bottom-right corner opens a small chat window with PHILSARBot, and ✕ closes it. Click PHILSARBot's name at the top of that window to open the full AI Assistant page.
- PHILSARBot answers questions about cattle reproduction and health, PHILSAR, and how to use this portal. When an answer uses the Learning Modules, the modules it used are listed under the reply.

## Admin Panel: managing user accounts (Admins and Sub Admins)

- Admin Panel → Users lists the accounts. Switch between "All Accounts" and "Archived" with the buttons above the table.
- A System Admin (role "Admin") can add an account with the "Add New User Account" form (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Password, Role and Farm / Organization, then "+ Save Account"), change someone's role (they're notified by bell and email), Deactivate or Activate an account (a deactivated account can't sign in), click "Request deletion" to remove an account, and Restore archived accounts.
- A Sub Admin sees the list as View only, but approves or rejects deletion requests. An approved account is archived, not erased, so it can be restored later.
- The roles are Livestock Manager, Farmer, Veterinarian, Extension Worker, Secretary, Sub Admin and Admin.

## Admin Panel: adding or editing a Learning Module (Admins and Sub Admins)

- Admin Panel → Modules. Fill in "Add Educational Module" (Module Title, Description, Topic, Cover Image and Content), then click "+ Create Module". To change a module, edit it from the list and click "Save Changes".
- The Cover Image must be landscape (wider than it is tall).
- Content uses Markdown. Each line that starts with "## " begins a new lesson, and "Insert Image" adds a picture to the content.
- PHILSARBot and Decision Support use the new lessons as soon as the module is saved.

## Admin Panel: scheduling seminars and managing certificates (Admins and Sub Admins)

- Admin Panel → Meetings. Fill in "Schedule Virtual Seminar" (Seminar Title, Expert Host Name, Meeting Type (Seminar or Regular Meeting), Who Can Join (Regular Meetings only), Date & Time, Status and an optional Recording Link), then click "+ Schedule Seminar".
- In the list, Mark as Live, Mark as Upcoming and Mark as Ended change a session's status. There are also Edit, View registrants and Delete.
- "Manage certificates" shows each attendee's time and certificate. Click "Grant" to give one manually, "Revoke" to cancel it, or "Grant again" to restore a revoked one (the same certificate and QR code become valid again).
- The minutes of attendance needed for an automatic certificate are set in the Settings tab.

## Admin Panel: Announcements, settings and activity log (System Admin only)

- Announcements tab: upload the Announcements page's banner photos with "Upload Background Photo" (landscape photos only), reorder or remove them, and post announcements (Title, Body and an optional Photo, then "+ Post Announcement"; they can be edited or deleted later).
- Settings tab: "Minutes of Attendance Required for Automatic Certificate" and the certificate's design (Certificate Title, Attendance Statement, Footer / Organization Line, Primary Color, Accent Color and an optional Custom Background). "Preview Certificate" shows a sample, and "Save Settings" applies the changes.
- Activity Log tab: a record of sign-ins, failed logins, lockouts, role and status changes, Decision Support runs, chatbot questions, seminar activity and admin actions, with filters and a search box.
